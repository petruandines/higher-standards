Who we are

About Petru & Inés

We are Petru and Inés, a young team developing an aviation service under the name Petru & Inés – Higher Standards. We believe aircraft care should feel professional, dependable and easy to arrange.

We serve clients throughout Romania, with particular focus on Bucharest and the surrounding area. If your aircraft is based elsewhere, contact us and we can discuss the location, scope and scheduling.

Our goal is to become the kind of partner an owner or operator feels comfortable contacting again: punctual, discreet, careful with materials and attentive to the overall presentation of the aircraft.

Selected products
