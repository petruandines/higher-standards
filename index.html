Petru & Inés

Higher standards, from the first touchpoint to the final check.

We are Petru & Inés, a small independent team focused on careful aircraft cleaning and detailing. We work with private owners, flight schools, operators, clubs and aviation businesses that value a clean, well-kept aircraft and straightforward communication.

Our work is mobile and appointment-based. We travel throughout Romania, with priority availability in Bucharest and the surrounding area. Every job is discussed in advance so we can arrive prepared for the aircraft, the location and the requested work.

Fly Cleaner. Fly Higher.

We bring the cleaning products, materials and equipment needed for the agreed service. Special access, site requirements or unusual aircraft materials are confirmed before arrival.

Services

Aircraft care with a surface-by-surface approach.

Not every aircraft surface should be treated the same way. We select the process and product according to the material, condition and scope agreed with the owner or operator.

01

Cabin cleaning & detailing

Careful cleaning of cabin surfaces, trim, touchpoints, storage areas and other accessible interior elements.

02

Leather, vinyl & seating care

Cleaning and conditioning selected for aviation seating materials, with attention to finish and long-term appearance.

03

Carpets & upholstery

Targeted cleaning for aircraft carpets and textile surfaces, including stain-focused work where appropriate.

04

Cockpit & avionics surfaces

Controlled cleaning of displays, instruments and cockpit touchpoints using products intended for sensitive aviation surfaces.

05

Windshield & transparencies

Streak-conscious cleaning for compatible aircraft transparencies, with product choice adapted to glass, acrylic or polycarbonate surfaces.

06

Exterior detail support

Selected exterior cleaning tasks, including insect removal and detail work on compatible surfaces, agreed in advance.

Our approach

Prepared, careful and easy to coordinate.

We keep the process simple for the owner while treating the aircraft as an aircraft — not as a car or a generic interior.

01

Scope first

We clarify aircraft type, requested areas, condition, access and location before the visit.

02

Surface-aware selection

Products and tools are chosen around the material and the task, with aviation-specific options where applicable.

03

Controlled execution

We work methodically around trim, stitching, displays, transparencies and other sensitive areas.

04

Final review

We finish with a visual check and review the completed scope with the client whenever possible.

Important:Cleaning is performed only within the agreed scope. Maintenance, disassembly and technical servicing remain outside our service unless separately arranged with appropriately authorised personnel.
